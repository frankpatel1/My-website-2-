1. Introduction

TrustNexus Technologies ("TrustNexus," "we," "our," or "us") respects the privacy of individuals who visit our website, contact our team, request quotations, engage our services, or interact with our business.

This Privacy Policy explains how we may collect, use, store, disclose, and protect personal information in connection with our website and business operations, including our CCTV surveillance, access control, AI analytics, fire and life-safety, network infrastructure, and maintenance services.

We aim to handle personal information responsibly, transparently, and in accordance with applicable laws.

2. Scope of This Policy

This Policy applies to:

  • Visitors to our website.
  • Individuals who submit inquiries or request quotations.
  • Customers, prospective customers, suppliers, and business representatives.
  • Individuals who communicate with us regarding projects, installation, support, or maintenance.
  • Personal information processed through our business systems, where TrustNexus determines the purpose and means of processing.

Where TrustNexus installs or maintains systems that process personal information on behalf of a customer, the customer may be the primary party responsible for determining how that information is collected and used. The applicable project agreement and data-processing arrangements will govern those responsibilities.

3. Information We May Collect

3.1 Contact and identification information

We may collect your name, business email address, telephone number, job title, company name, business address, and other details you voluntarily provide.

3.2 Inquiry and project information

We may collect information relating to quotation requests, project requirements, site surveys, technical specifications, correspondence, purchase orders, service requests, and customer feedback.

3.3 Website and technical information

Depending on our website configuration, we may collect IP addresses, browser type, device information, pages visited, approximate location derived from technical information, timestamps, and website diagnostic logs. Cookies or similar technologies may be used where configured and permitted by applicable law.

3.4 Customer and service information

For service delivery, we may process authorized contact details, equipment inventories, maintenance records, fault reports, service tickets, system configuration information, and technical logs.

3.5 Surveillance and access-control information

Certain customer projects may involve video footage, photographs, facial images, access logs, credential identifiers, visitor records, alarm events, and AI-generated security alerts. TrustNexus will access or process such information only as authorized by the applicable customer agreement, project scope, and relevant law. This Policy does not independently authorize surveillance or biometric processing.

4. How We Use Information

We may use personal information to:

  • Respond to inquiries and prepare quotations.
  • Assess project requirements and conduct authorized site surveys.
  • Deliver installation, integration, commissioning, and maintenance services.
  • Provide customer support and manage service requests.
  • Coordinate authorized engineering activities and project communications.
  • Maintain business, accounting, and contractual records.
  • Protect our website, networks, personnel, and business systems.
  • Investigate suspected fraud, misuse, or security incidents.
  • Meet applicable legal and regulatory obligations.
  • Improve our website, services, and operational processes where legally permitted.

We will not intentionally use personal information for purposes incompatible with the purpose for which it was collected without an appropriate legal basis.

5. Legal Basis and Consent

Where applicable law requires consent or another specific legal basis, we will process personal information on that basis.

Depending on the circumstances, processing may be necessary to respond to a request, perform a contract, comply with a legal obligation, protect legitimate business interests where legally permitted, or carry out another lawful purpose.

Where consent is required, individuals may withdraw it through an appropriate channel. Withdrawal does not automatically invalidate processing lawfully carried out before withdrawal.

6. CCTV, AI Analytics and Biometric Information

TrustNexus provides technology integration services that may include video surveillance, AI-powered analytics, facial recognition, and access-control systems. For these deployments:

  • The customer or facility operator is generally responsible for establishing the lawful purpose of surveillance and determining appropriate notices, permissions, and retention periods.
  • Cameras and analytics should be configured according to the approved project scope and applicable legal requirements.
  • Access to footage, biometric information, and access logs should be restricted to authorized personnel.
  • Data collection, storage, sharing, and deletion should follow documented customer instructions and applicable law.
  • AI-generated alerts should be treated in accordance with the system's capabilities and limitations. They should not automatically be assumed to be accurate or conclusive.
  • Where TrustNexus acts as a service provider processing customer data, we will follow the applicable contract and authorized instructions.

Customers remain responsible for obtaining any permissions, notices, consents, or authorizations required for their facilities and use cases.

7. Sharing of Information

We may share information with:

  • Authorized employees and project personnel.
  • Technology vendors, OEMs, and technical service providers where required for an agreed service.
  • Hosting, communication, IT support, and business administration providers.
  • Professional advisers, auditors, and insurers where appropriate.
  • Government authorities, regulators, or law-enforcement agencies where legally required or otherwise lawfully permitted.
  • A successor entity in connection with a lawful business restructuring or transfer, subject to applicable privacy requirements.

We do not sell personal information to third parties for their independent marketing purposes. Where third-party providers process personal information on our behalf, appropriate contractual and security measures are implemented according to the nature of the processing and applicable law.

8. Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the relevant purpose, subject to applicable legal, contractual, operational, and security requirements. Retention periods may differ for:

  • Website inquiries and business correspondence.
  • Customer and project records.
  • Invoices and statutory business records.
  • Technical logs and maintenance tickets.
  • CCTV footage and access-control records managed under customer instructions.

Where TrustNexus maintains a customer's surveillance or access-control system, retention periods should be defined in the relevant project documentation or customer policy. When information is no longer required, it is securely deleted, anonymized, or otherwise handled as permitted by law and the applicable agreement.

9. Information Security

We aim to apply reasonable technical and organizational safeguards appropriate to the information processed. Depending on the system and project, safeguards may include access restrictions, authentication controls, encryption where supported, network security measures, backups, logging, software updates, and secure configuration practices.

No website, network, or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure. We therefore cannot promise absolute protection against every possible incident.

10. Data Breaches and Security Incidents

Where an incident affects personal information under our responsibility, we will assess the incident and take appropriate containment, investigation, remediation, and notification steps as required by applicable law and contractual obligations.

Where TrustNexus processes information on behalf of a customer, we will follow the agreed incident-reporting procedure and applicable legal requirements.

11. Cross-Border Data Transfers

Information may be processed or stored outside the location where it was collected if permitted by applicable law and required for an authorized service. Where cross-border processing occurs, appropriate safeguards and contractual arrangements will be applied as required by the relevant legal framework.

12. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on applicable law and the circumstances, you may have the right to:

  • Request information about the personal data we process.
  • Request correction or updating of inaccurate information.
  • Request deletion of information where legally applicable.
  • Withdraw consent where processing depends on consent.
  • Raise a grievance or privacy complaint.
  • Exercise other rights available under applicable law.

Requests may require identity verification. Certain information may need to be retained where legally required or where another lawful basis applies. Where surveillance data is controlled by a customer facility operator, requests relating to that data may need to be directed to the relevant operator.

13. Children's Privacy

Our business services are primarily directed toward organizations and business customers. We do not knowingly collect children's personal information through our website for an independent purpose unless legally permitted and appropriately authorized. Where our systems are deployed in schools, healthcare facilities, or other environments involving children, the customer must establish appropriate lawful processing, notices, permissions, and safeguards.

14. Cookies and Website Analytics

Our website may use essential cookies, analytics tools, or similar technologies, depending on the configuration in use. Where required, we will provide appropriate notices and consent controls. Visitors may also manage cookies through their browser settings, although disabling certain cookies may affect website functionality.

15. Third-Party Websites

Our website may contain links to external websites, OEM resources, or third-party services. TrustNexus does not control the privacy practices of independent third parties. Visitors should review the applicable privacy policies before submitting personal information to those services.

16. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in our services, technology, business operations, or applicable law. The revised version will be published with an updated revision date. Where required, we will provide additional notice or obtain consent for material changes.

18. Governing Law

This Policy will be interpreted in accordance with applicable Indian law, including the Information Technology Act, 2000, the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, and any other mandatory privacy laws applicable to the relevant processing activity.
