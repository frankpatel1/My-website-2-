Heavy Manufacturing & Industrial Plant ELV

HAZARDOUS ATMOSPHERES

ATEX / IECEx Explosion-Proof Surveillance

Heavy-gauge AISI 316L stainless steel explosion-proof camera enclosures engineered for Zone 1 and Zone 2 gas/dust hazards. Built-in wiper blades, sunshields, and continuous optical purge systems keep lenses crystal clear.

PERIMETER DEFENCE

Thermal Radar Perimeter Protection

Combine long-range cooled/uncooled thermal imaging with 77 GHz radar tracking along vast refinery, chemical, or automotive boundaries. Automatic PTZ visual slew-to-cue verifies human incursions while ignoring wildlife and fog.

Industrial PAGA & Toxic Gas Warning

Plant-wide Public Address and General Alarm (PAGA) systems engineered for high-decibel boiler rooms and noisy turbine bays. High-output strobe sounders and automated zone paging trigger immediately upon toxic gas or fire alarm.
