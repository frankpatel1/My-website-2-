VENDOR-NEUTRAL DISCIPLINE

How We Choose Technology

We hold no contractual obligation to push a single brand. Our engineers select hardware based on physical facility physics, cybersecurity resilience, and long-term lifecycle viability.

Criterion 01

Fit to Your Site

Optical specs, explosion-proof ratings, cleanroom air pressure limits, and temperature tolerances are mapped directly to your physical environment.

Criterion 02

Interoperability First

Every camera, controller, and panel must support open standard protocols (ONVIF, BACnet, Modbus, MQTT, Wiegand/OSDP) to avoid proprietary vendor lock-in.

Criterion 03

Lifecycle & Service Support

We partner exclusively with OEMs that commit to long-term spare part availability, Indian RMA service hubs, and routine firmware security updates.
