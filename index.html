Facility & IT Engineering FAQ

Clear answers to common questions about brownfield integrations, response commitments, and AMC 2.0 coverage.

Yes, absolutely. We specialize in brownfield modernizations. Using open ONVIF standards, hybrid RTSP streams, and edge AI analytic gateway appliances, we can ingest feeds from legacy analog or older IP cameras into a unified VMS platform without requiring a wholesale replacement of functional hardware. We certify existing copper and fiber runs using calibrated Fluke DSX-8000 analyzers before re-utilizing them.

Traditional AMCs only dispatch a technician after a catastrophic failure. TrustNexus AMC 2.0 is predictive and SLA-governed. It includes quarterly firmware hardening to block CVE zero-day exploits, optical lens recalibration, backup power load tests, Fluke fiber OTDR baseline audits, standby emergency hot-swappable spares in Ahmedabad, and guaranteed 2-hour on-site dispatch backed by financial penalty SLAs.

For standard corporate facilities or small industrial units (under 50,000 sq.ft.), a preliminary survey is typically completed in 4 to 6 hours. For large manufacturing campuses, pharma facilities, or multi-story towers, our senior system architects spend 1 to 2 days conducting physical lux level audits, conduit pathway checks, Wi-Fi heatmapping, and fire panel zoning. You receive a full written survey report within 72 hours.

Within the Ahmedabad urban area (including Sanand, Changodar, Naroda, and SP Ring Road), our guaranteed emergency on-site SLA is under 2 hours for Level 1 critical failures (e.g. fire panel outage or main server room access failure). Across broader Gujarat industrial hubs (Dahej, Vadodara, Ankleshwar, Rajkot, Hazira), we provide a 4 to 6-hour SLA with dedicated regional mobile engineering vehicles.

Every fire detection and voice evacuation system we deploy conforms strictly to National Building Code (NBC) 2016 Part 4 and NFPA 72 standards. We coordinate with local chief fire officers (CFO) in Gujarat for initial drawing approvals, perform hydro-testing and calibrated detector smoke-spray tests, and provide all necessary Form B certification documentation required for municipal Fire NOC issuance.
